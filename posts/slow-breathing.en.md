Slow breathing is one of the commonly suggested ways to calm down. This post looks at which studies are behind the advice and where its limits are. The short version: slow breathing can help you relax, but the evidence is still limited and rests on small studies.

## What does slow breathing do in the body?

A systematic review published in *Frontiers in Human Neuroscience* in 2018 found only 15 studies that met its criteria out of 2,461 abstracts. In these studies, slow breathing techniques raised heart rate variability and the heart rhythm linked to breathing. On EEG, alpha power went up and theta power went down. On the psychological side, comfort, relaxation and alertness went up, and signs of anxiety, anger and depression went down. This was seen in healthy people.

The authors say the mechanism is still debated. In other words, we observe what happens, but we do not fully know why.

## A five-minute breathing exercise

A study published in *Cell Reports Medicine* in 2023 asked a more practical question: is five minutes a day enough? Participants spent a month at home doing a randomly assigned exercise: "cyclic sighing", which focuses on long exhales, box breathing, fast breathing with holds, or, as a comparison, mindfulness meditation.

- Breathing exercises, especially cyclic sighing with its focus on exhaling, improved mood more than mindfulness meditation did.
- Breathing rate also fell more.
- The authors say five minutes a day of cyclic sighing looks promising for stress management.

The study has limits: it was run remotely from home, and the mood measures rely largely on people's own reports. It is also a single trial.

## So how is it done?

This is a suggestion, not a proven recipe.

1. Sit comfortably and let your shoulders drop.
2. Breathe in through your nose. If you like, add a short second sip of air to fill your lungs a little more.
3. Breathe out slowly and for long, through your mouth or nose. Make the exhale longer than the inhale. That is the point the study emphasizes.
4. Keep going for five minutes.
5. If you feel dizzy or uncomfortable, go back to normal breathing and stop. If you have a breathing or heart condition, ask your doctor first.

## In Serin

The breathing meditation in Serin has Calm, 4-7-8 and Box rhythms. Box breathing was one of the methods tried in the 2023 study; 4-7-8 was not part of it. Serin is not a treatment. If anxiety or sleep problems continue, talk to a health professional.
